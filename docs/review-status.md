## Incremental transcription and roadmap boundary

Closed five-minute capture files now support bounded local recognition, durable checkpoints, final reuse and boundary-context reconciliation. Open or unacknowledged files, cloud selection, changed audio and saved archives cannot enter this path. Final text alone is sent to the Gateway. Rotation/backpressure/concurrency/cancellation/boundary tests establish source behavior. [Incremental transcription](incremental-transcription.md) and [streaming assessment](streaming-assessment.md) state the limits.

True live streaming and provisional-text UI are **not implemented**. The pinned engine was inspected; CPU/ANE, latency, battery and accuracy require real measurements. Incremental closed-file recognition must pass live acceptance before streaming is considered. Draft Gateway notes are also **not implemented**: the review made them conditional; they would need separate idempotent revision identities and cannot overwrite finals. Other meeting providers remain disabled by the user's explicit scope. These are roadmap proposals, not completed features or reasons to weaken existing source contracts.
